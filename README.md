
# Reeltic Resources

Reeltic helps local businesses showcase authentic customer experiences through short video reviews.

**Website:** https://www.reeltic.com/

## What are customer video reviews?

Short, genuine videos let customers share their experiences in their own words. Businesses can use these reviews to provide prospective customers with more personal insight into their services.

## Resources for local businesses

- Encourage customers to share honest, voluntary feedback.
- Make the review submission process simple and accessible.
- Disclose rewards and creator assistance clearly.
- Respect customer consent and privacy.
- Never fabricate reviews or suppress negative feedback.

## About Reeltic

Reeltic's business model focuses on authentic customer video reviews, with optional in-person video shoots and coupon subscriptions for participating businesses.

Learn more on the [official Reeltic website](https://www.reeltic.com/).
